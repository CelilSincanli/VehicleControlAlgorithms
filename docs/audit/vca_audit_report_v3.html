Why Kinematic and Dynamic traces look the same

In the gallery the two models' traces overlap. They are genuinely different runs: every Dynamic run has 0.35–0.83 m/s of sideslip velocity (always 0 for Kinematic) and its own capped speed, and the traces are 0.07–0.47 m apart, below one pixel at gallery scale. Three things make them so close:

  1. The speed cap keeps Dynamic runs below the tire limit. Peak lateral acceleration in the baseline is 0.8–2.6 m/s², a quarter of the friction limit. With the cap switched off the models separate and Dynamic runs fail: 35/36 clean at 5 m/s, 29/36 at 6 m/s, 25/36 at 8 m/s (Kinematic stays clean).
  2. Both vehicle files are neutral-steer. Cornering stiffness is coeff × axle load with the same coefficient front and rear, so the understeer gradient Wf/Cf − Wr/Cr is exactly 0. Below the friction limit the linear-tire model then turns on the same circle as the kinematic one (open-loop test: same radius within 2 % up to 8 m/s). Near the limit it differs strongly: at a constant 0.2 rad command, Car 01 runs an 18 % wider circle at 12 m/s and 85 % wider at 15 m/s; Bus 01 32 % wider at 15 m/s.
  3. Closed-loop controllers correct the difference. Each controller steers back to the path every 1/60 s, so the remaining gap is the tracking error, 0.1–0.5 m.

New finding B31 (not fixed): real cars understeer (front cornering stiffness per unit load lower than rear). Giving the tire block separate front and rear coefficients would make the Dynamic model behave differently from Kinematic in normal driving, not only at the limit.

Method

All runs use tests/sim_harness.cpp, which links the project's real sources and the same simulation rules as the GUI (src/sim/sim_rules.cpp): fixed 1/60 s step, limited speed command, goal line, Failed on collision / off-map / timeout. Reproduce with ctest and python3 tests/run_matrix.py build results. The GUI was launched, not driven; the manual checks are in the chat summary.
